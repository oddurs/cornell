## Question

The starburst around a street light at a small aperture, the rainbow off a CD,
the fact that a lens cannot resolve detail below its diffraction limit no
matter how well ground.

## Why it is here, last

This is the boundary of the approximation the entire project is built on. Rays
are what Maxwell's equations reduce to when every feature is large compared
with a wavelength, and diffraction is exactly what happens when that stops
being true.

Modelling it properly means not using rays, which means not being this
project.

## What would settle it

Nothing. This item exists to be the honest terminus of the list: here is where
the model ends, and it ends because of an assumption stated in the first
header a reader opens.
