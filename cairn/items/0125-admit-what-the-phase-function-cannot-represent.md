Henyey-Greenstein is symmetric about the forward axis and has one parameter.
Real scattering is not: a water droplet produces a rainbow and a glory, which
are strongly wavelength-dependent angular features, and no one-parameter
symmetric fit can produce either.

What it costs: no rainbows, from any medium, ever, until Mie arrives in v1.2.
