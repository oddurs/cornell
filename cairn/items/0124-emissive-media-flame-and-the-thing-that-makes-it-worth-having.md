A medium that emits as well as scatters. Blackbody emission at a temperature
is a spectrum by Planck's law — which is a genuine law, derivable, and which
gives the colour of a flame or a filament for free from one number.

Another place where the spectral decision pays: a blackbody at 1800 K has a
colour, and it is not a parameter.
