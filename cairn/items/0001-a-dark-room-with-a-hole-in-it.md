The oldest instrument there is: a sealed box, a small aperture, and the observation that light travels in straight lines so a pinhole sorts it by direction.

By the end of this there is a program that writes an image, and the image is wrong in every way except its geometry. No shading, no colour, no light — a ray leaves the aperture, meets a sphere or it does not, and the film records which.

It exists to settle the conventions everything after inherits: that the inside of this program is SI, that a colour is a spectrum, and that an image file is forty bytes of header and some numbers.
