The thesis.

Gold is a table of complex refractive index measured by Johnson and Christy in 1972, and the yellow falls out of Fresnel. Swap the table for copper and the reflection turns pink because of physics. Swap it for aluminium and it goes neutral.

If a material ever needs a tint multiplied on afterwards to look right, the model has quietly stopped being true and the project is over. Every milestone before this one exists to make this one checkable.
