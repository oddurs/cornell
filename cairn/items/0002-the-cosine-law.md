Lambert, Augsburg, 1760. A surface seen at an angle presents less of itself, by exactly the cosine, and what looks like a property of matte paint is a fact about projected area.

This is where the rendering equation arrives and where the program stops being a ray caster and becomes an integrator. The first image with light in it, and the first noise.

It is also where the estimator discipline is set down and must never be relaxed: every sample carries the density it was drawn from, and the ratio is written out in full at the point of use.
