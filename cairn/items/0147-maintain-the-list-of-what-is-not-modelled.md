House rule 7 is a standing obligation, not a one-time task.

Every milestone accumulates admissions. They belong in the headers where a
reader will meet them, collected in the README, and visible here as
`cairn list --view honesty`.

An unstated simplification is a lie; a stated one is a design decision. The
difference is maintenance.
