Until now light has travelled between surfaces unchanged, which is an assumption about vacuum that no photograph was ever taken in.

Absorption by Beer and Lambert, scattering by a phase function Henyey and Greenstein fitted to interstellar dust in 1941 and which the graphics field has used for skin and milk and cloud ever since — with that provenance written down, because it is a warning as much as a credit.
