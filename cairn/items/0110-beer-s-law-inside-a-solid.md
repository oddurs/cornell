Thick "clear" glass is green because iron impurities absorb red, and the
absorption is exponential in path length. This is the first participating
medium in the project and the simplest one — absorption only, no scattering —
which makes it a good rehearsal for v1.1.

- [ ] The coefficient is spectral, and the green falls out
- [ ] A wedge of glass, thin to thick, shows the gradient
