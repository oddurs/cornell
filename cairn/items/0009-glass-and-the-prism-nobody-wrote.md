Refraction, total internal reflection, absorption through a medium, and the payoff of five milestones of spectral discipline: the index of refraction depends on wavelength, so white light entering a prism leaves it spread into a spectrum.

There is no prism model. There is no dispersion parameter. It is Snell, evaluated per wavelength, on a Sellmeier fit to measured glass.
