Stop generating rays through a mathematical pinhole and start tracing them
through actual surfaces: a list of radii, thicknesses, glasses and apertures,
which is what a lens prescription is and what every patent contains.

Everything photographers have vocabulary for follows, and none of it needs a
parameter: focus breathing, field curvature, the way a fast lens goes soft at
the edges wide open and sharpens down.

- [ ] The glasses are the Sellmeier fits already in the project
