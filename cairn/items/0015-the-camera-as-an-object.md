A pinhole has no aberration because it has no glass. Every real camera is a stack of ground surfaces, and everything photographers have vocabulary for — bokeh, coma, vignetting, the way a fast lens renders an out-of-focus highlight as a disc with a bright rim — is that stack being imperfect in a specific way.

Trace rays through the actual elements. The bokeh is the shape of the aperture because it is the shape of the aperture.
