A spectrum is a function of wavelength and a monitor has three primaries, and the bridge between them is a table of numbers fitted to seventeen people in London in 1931.

Until now there has been no colour anywhere in the program, only radiance. This milestone builds the two — and only two — places where that stops being true: the observer that collapses a spectrum to tristimulus, and the transfer function that makes a display out of it.

Everything after this can be looked at.
