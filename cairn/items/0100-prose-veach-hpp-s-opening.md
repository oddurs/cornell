## What it has to explain

The problem before the solution: two images at the same sample count, one
lit by a small bright lamp and one by a large dim panel, rendered with each
strategy, so a reader sees the catastrophe on both sides before being shown
the thing that fixes it.

Veach's thesis is the most consequential document in the field and the MIS
chapter is the most reusable idea in it. Say so.

## What it must not do

Present the weights as a formula. They are a consequence of a requirement, and
the requirement is one sentence long.
