Light no longer travels unchanged between surfaces. It is absorbed, scattered
out of the beam, scattered into it from elsewhere, and possibly emitted.

The header should put the new equation beside the old one and mark which term
is which, because the whole of v1.1 through v1.3 is consequences of those four
terms and a reader who has the equation can predict what is coming.
