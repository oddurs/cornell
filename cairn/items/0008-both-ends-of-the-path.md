Veach, 1995. Two strategies that are each terrible in the case where the other is excellent, combined so that the combination is never much worse than the better of them.

Light sampling makes small bright sources possible. BSDF sampling makes sharp highlights possible. Multiple importance sampling is what lets a renderer have both without choosing, and it is only reachable because every sample has carried its density since v0.2.
