Louis Henyey and Jesse Greenstein, 1941, fitting the scattering of light by
dust between the stars. It is a one-parameter curve with no derivation behind
it, chosen because it was analytically convenient and fitted the astronomical
observations.

It is now used, unchanged, for skin, milk, marble, cloud, smoke and fog.

House rule 8's best warning: the credit is also the caveat. A function fitted
to interstellar dust has no business describing human tissue and does it
tolerably well anyway, and a reader should be told that rather than left to
assume a derivation exists.
