What happens when light enters a surface, scatters a few hundred times inside it, and leaves somewhere else. It is why an ear is red with the sun behind it and why marble does not look like painted plaster.

Random-walk subsurface scattering, which is the honest version, with the diffusion approximations named and their failure cases stated rather than hidden.
