## What it has to explain

That the whole program is Maxwell's equations in the limit where every feature
is large compared with a wavelength, that this is a choice, and what the choice
costs by name: polarisation, fluorescence, diffraction, interference.

A reader who wants to render a soap bubble should find out in the first file
they open, not in the third week.
