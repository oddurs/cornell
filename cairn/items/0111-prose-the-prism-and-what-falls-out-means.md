## What it has to explain

The strongest statement of the project's whole argument, made at the point
where it is most visible.

Newton put a prism in a sunbeam in 1666 and argued that white light is
composite. This program reproduces that experiment, and the reproduction
consists of a shape, a glass name from a catalogue, and a light. The rainbow
is not in the code. It is in the coefficients, which somebody measured, and in
Snell's law, which is older than all of it.

## What it must not do

Overclaim. The image will have some noise and some gamut trouble at the
saturated end, and both should be stated where the picture is shown.
